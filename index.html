Feed structure

The published GTFS contains:

agency.txt, stops.txt, routes.txt, trips.txt, stop_times.txt, calendar_dates.txt, feed_info.txt

Processing

The feed is generated automatically from the Italo NeTEx source. Two-train-number combination routes are removed, route variants are merged into a single route number, and service IDs are enriched with their actual date range and weekday pattern.
